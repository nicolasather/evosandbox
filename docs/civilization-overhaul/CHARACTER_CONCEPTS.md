# First six citizen concepts

| Archetype | Silhouette / prop | Gameplay read |
|---|---|---|
| Gatherer | Moss-green tunic, woven satchel, berry sprig | Food and wild-resource collection |
| Hunter | Dark ochre wrap, short spear, travel stance | Meat and scouting risk |
| Builder | Clay apron, mallet, carried plank | Construction progress and material demand |
| Toolmaker | Indigo work vest, stone hammer, bright belt accent | Workshop efficiency and discovery bridge |
| Elder | Pale hair, layered shawl, walking staff | Hints, history, teaching |
| Child | Smaller body, soft green shirt, quick idle bounce | Household life and future workforce |

## Hint/dialogue examples

- `hint_storage_exposed_observation`: “The rain keeps soaking the food we leave outside.”
- `hint_storage_suggestion`: “We should find a way to keep supplies dry.”
- `hint_storage_guidance`: “A covered storage place might solve that.”
