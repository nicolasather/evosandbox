# Historical research notes

These references inform plausibility and vocabulary, not a rigid tech tree. Existing discovery data remains the authority for valid combinations.

| Topic | Reference | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| Early agriculture | https://www.britannica.com/topic/agriculture | Farming is a capability with seasonal labour and land constraints, not an instant era button. |
| Food storage | https://www.fao.org/3/y4893e/y4893e00.htm | Storage reduces loss through shelter, dryness and handling; it does not create food. |
| Pottery and containers | https://www.metmuseum.org/toah/hd/potf/hd_potf.htm | Containers support storage and cooking chains while requiring material and specialist work. |
| Early metallurgy | https://www.britannica.com/technology/metallurgy | Tool improvements should require inputs, heat/work capacity and learned practice. |
| Ancient trade | https://www.metmuseum.org/toah/hd/trade/hd_trade.htm | Trade begins as local exchange of surplus for scarce goods, with distance and trust costs. |

## Simplification guardrails

Do not represent history as a single inevitable ladder. Keep alternate plausible routes, show uncertainty, and make every capability change a visible settlement decision with a cost and consequence.
