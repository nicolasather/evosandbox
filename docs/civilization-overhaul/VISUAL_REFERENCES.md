## Character-specific study notes

- Stardew Valley / ConcernedApe: use clothing and hair blocks to communicate personality at small scale; do not imitate its character designs.
- Kingdom / Raw Fury and Fury Studios: preserve strong silhouette separation against busy terrain; character contrast should survive the same game zoom as the environment.
- Kenney Pixel Platformer / Kenney: keep sheet rows and frame spacing mechanically predictable; our 32x48 contract is original to this project.
- OpenGameArt CC0 resources: treat license metadata as part of the asset, even when the project asset itself is original.
- itch.io CC0 character search: compare frame-count conventions only; do not import a pack without reviewing its individual creator license.

## Original direction derived

Use the concept board’s warm natural palette and top-left light, but make our characters more grounded and human-scale than fantasy sprites: plum-brown outlines, 32px frame contract, role prop silhouettes, and no copied character designs.
